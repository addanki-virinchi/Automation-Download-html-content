# Refer a hero. Earn the rewards. Build the community.

When you help another contractor discover JobNimbus, everyone wins. You get paid. They get organized. Together, you both build stronger businesses.

### How does it work?

  1. Join our referral program via our [referral dashboard](https://jobnimbus.partnerstack.com/?group=customers):   
  
Create a login in our referral management platform and receive a custom referral link that will track sign-ups for timely payouts.
  2. Share your link: Share your custom link with any contractor!
  3. You get rewarded: Once they sign up for JobNimbus, you earn $50 cash.*  
  
Bonus Levels:  
3 referrals = $100 bonus + swag  
5 referrals = $150 bonus + perks

### Why join the program?

Becoming contractor heroes happens when great software and great community overlap.   
  
When new users join JobNimbus through a referral, they’re stepping into a network that knows how to get things done. They learn the ropes faster, adopt the tools quicker, and start seeing results sooner—all with support from people who’ve been there.   
  
And sure, the referral bonus is a nice perk, but the real reward is helping another contractor level up.
