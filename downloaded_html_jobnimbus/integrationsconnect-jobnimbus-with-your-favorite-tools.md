### Roofing Contractors Association of Texas

The Roofing Contractors Association of Texas (RCAT) is a statewide trade association of roofing and waterproofing contractors. This group of professionals collectively represents the voice of the roofing and waterproofing industry in Texas.

### Midwest Roofing Contractors Association

The Midwest Roofing Contractors Association (MRCA) is a regional trade association dedicated to the needs of roofing contractors, and is often referred to as "The Contractors Advocate." MRCA's goal is to serve the roofing contractor community with education and resources to help them grow & prosper.
