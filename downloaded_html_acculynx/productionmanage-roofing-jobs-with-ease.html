Roofing production management features

Manage and streamline every aspect of production

The production features in AccuLynx’s project management software give you greater control and visibility into your roofing jobs. From simplifying crew scheduling to streamlining material ordering, AccuLynx makes it easier to stay ahead of potential problems and keep roofing jobs on track.

See Demo

Production management tools that keep roofing jobs on schedule and in budget

Digital job files

Manage every aspect of a project from the AccuLynx job file and keep all of your information—including estimates, contracts, documents, photos, measurements, and more—together, in one place.

Messages & activity feed

See what’s happening at a glance with a live activity feed that eliminates the need to track down updates. You can also use the job’s message board to tag team members and keep them updated.

Production calendar

Access all of the company’s appointments, deliveries, and scheduled labor from a single production calendar. Use filters and color-codes to quickly find what you need and make updates on the fly.

Labor manager

Organize subcontractors and crews the way you want, giving you better control over labor availability and tools to run more efficiently. Capture contact details, maintain up-to-date records, and control what job information is shared.

Scheduling

Schedule crews for jobs in just a few clicks. When you need to make changes, team members are automatically notified so everyone knows what’s happening.

Ordering

Build material orders quickly and place them directly to QXO, SRS, and ABC Supply without leaving AccuLynx. Use the Order Manager to stay on top of every order.

Roofing project management software is a combination of tools that help roofing contractors manage their projects, customers, and teams. AccuLynx has all the project management features contractors need to move projects from the initial lead to the final invoice.
