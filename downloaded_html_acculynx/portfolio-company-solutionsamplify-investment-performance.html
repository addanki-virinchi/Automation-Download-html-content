AccuLynx helps private equity-backed roofing companies scale operations and drive higher profits

Simplify business operations

AccuLynx helps private equity firms scale roofing companies faster by centralizing workflows, automating tasks, and ensuring consistency across businesses. With features like streamlined project tracking, enhanced communication, and real-time updates, AccuLynx helps your roofing companies stay on top of operations and maintain quality—no matter how big your portfolio gets.

Leverage your data

AccuLynx equips private equity firms with real-time financial tracking, streamlined budget monitoring, and seamless integrations with accounting software like QuickBooks and Sage Intacct. With AccuLynx, PE firms have access to real-time portfolio-wide data, advanced querying, and custom analytics—helping them maximize returns, standardize reporting, and scale smarter across multiple entities.

The AccuLynx advantage

AccuLynx offers private equity-backed roofing companies a number of advantages that lead to real results.
Here’s what makes us different:

With over 15 years of experience, AccuLynx has helped thousands of roofing companies scale, averaging 32% higher job profits in one year. Our award-winning platform is trusted for its reliability and leadership in roofing business management.

At AccuLynx, we work directly with contractors to incorporate their feedback and evolve the software alongside our users. This approach offers private equity firms a solution tailored to meet the unique challenges their portfolio companies face in roofing.

AccuLynx has the most comprehensive reporting tools available for roofing businesses. By combining customizable reports, dashboards, and analytics into one system, AccuLynx gives private equity firms the ability to track progress, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

AccuLynx prioritizes your data, exceeding SaaS industry uptime standards. With two-factor authentication and real-time monitoring through third-party software like New Relic and DataDog, we ensure your data is always protected and accessible, so you can trust our software reliability anytime.

AccuLynx offers free, U.S.-based phone support with a large, dedicated team to help roofing companies maximize software use. We quickly respond to and resolve support tickets, ensuring smooth operations and no additional costs for fast, reliable assistance.
